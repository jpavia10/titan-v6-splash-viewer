# Obsidian — v4 intro attempt (preview, NOT gold)
Timed intro, 14.5 s. Tap PLAY first (the browser needs a tap before audio can start). Skip / mute / replay buttons.
0–1.5 s black → 1.2 s low swell → 1.5–5 s reveal (macro on the tip, cool rim sweep, then warm key + HDRI fade in) → 4.6 s glass ping
→ 5–8 s hold on the glass, slow push → 8.2 s crack hit + amber thin-edge glow pulse → 10.2 s title lands below the hero (sub-bass hit) → 12.4 s ping → hold to 14.5 s, ends on the frame with replay.
Craft: 420 flake scars + 500 edge nicks, planar clipping on a 12.6k-point lens hull; scratch/pit/ripple map; thin-edge emissive mask (per-vertex), no transmission;
iridescent thin film + sheen for Fresnel color between highlights; NaN/firefly clamp (fixed the v3 square-bloom artifact); 36 dust motes as fixed-pixel accents.
